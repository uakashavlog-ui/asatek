01 — What is a Solar Charge Controller, and why is it necessary?

A solar charge controller regulates the voltage and current coming from solar panels to protect batteries from overcharging and reverse current leakage. It acts as the intelligent hub balancing direct DC load diversion, bulk charging absorption, and float float levels for lead-acid and tubular batteries.

02 — What is the difference between PWM and MPPT charge controllers?

PWM is a direct pulse-width modulation switch ideal for 12V/24V matched systems, while MPPT utilizes DC-to-DC step down conversion for higher PV voltages (up to Voc 150V), extracting up to 30% more energy during cloudy and low-sunlight hours.

04 — How do I convert my regular UPS or inverter into a solar hybrid system?

Connect the ASATEK controller in parallel with the battery terminals of your regular UPS. The controller charges the battery during daytime, ensuring zero WAPDA consumption while keeping the battery fully cycled and ready for night load-shedding.
