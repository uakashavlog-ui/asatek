Frequently Asked Questions

help

Can't find your order details?

If you made a phone or offline dealership booking within the last 3 hours, courier dispatch data may take up to 4 hours to sync with the logistics backbone. Alternatively, please cross-check that you entered your 11-digit booking mobile without spaces.

edit_location

Need to change your delivery address?

Address modifications can only be processed before courier vehicle departure. WhatsApp our central warehouse dispatch team immediately with your order booking number to reroute the delivery.
