Engineered to Outlast Fragile Imported Alternatives

ASATEK is a specialized Pakistani manufacturer of industrial-grade solar charge controllers, UPS inverters, and multi-stage battery chargers. Born in Lahore's industrial heart, we engineer equipment with 100% copper winding, thick aluminum heat sinks, and rugged analog meters that outlast fragile imported consumer electronics.

When grid voltage plunges to 140V or spikes abruptly past 280V during transformer fluctuations, conventional imported units trip or fry their power stages. ASATEK designs hardware purposefully for indigenous realities: dual-layer anti-dust silicone conformal coatings, heavy-gauge steel enclosures, and modular replacement boards serviced within hours anywhere in the country.
