How Walk-In Warranty Claims Work

No endless waiting cycles. Bring your ASATEK device with its barcode sticker to any authorized hub across Pakistan for standardized three-stage resolution.

01

Instant Serial Scan & Load Test

Technician scans your unit barcode to verify national warranty records. Device undergoes immediate DC bench load testing to identify fault signatures.

02

Component Modular Swap

We use pre-tested sub-boards, genuine high-temp MOSFET arrays, and manufacturer-authorized firmware flashing. No makeshift soldering shortcuts.

03

Final Burn-In & Certification Handover

30-minute stress cycle under nominal rated current. Handover complete with automated digital warranty extension voucher.
