A solar charge controller regulates the voltage and current coming from your solar panels before feeding it into your batteries. It prevents battery overcharging during peak sunlight and stops reverse current drain at night, prolonging battery life by up to 3x.

check_circleOvercharge Cut-off: 14.4V check_circleNight Reverse Bleed: 0.00mA

PWM (Pulse Width Modulation) is a cost-effective, heavy-duty switch mechanism ideal for standard 12V and 24V solar setups. MPPT (Maximum Power Point Tracking) electronically tracks peak panel voltage and provides up to 15-30% extra efficiency in mismatched high-voltage solar arrays. ASATEK provides heavy-duty solid-state PWM controllers designed specifically for Pakistani climate and battery types.
