Safe Wiring Sequence

Always adhere to the sequential protocol to prevent internal high-voltage arcing across terminal gates:

  1. 1
    Connect Battery First:

    Allows internal sensing circuits to calibrate 12V baseline before PV exposure.

  2. 2
    Connect Solar PV Panels:

    Analog meters will smoothly deflect to indicate operational generation.

  3. 3
    Connect DC Load Output:

    Direct DC fans, light loops, or inverter trigger lines.
